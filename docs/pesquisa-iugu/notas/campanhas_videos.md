# iugu: campaigns, videos, customer cases, events and category contrast (research as of 2026-10-23)

How the videos were checked. I could not play any video with sound and picture together. Each video entry carries a label:
- **[T]** I read the full YouTube auto-caption transcript (Portuguese, machine-generated, so names are often misheard: "Hugo", "Yugo" or "Igor" = iugu).
- **[F]** I viewed frames: YouTube storyboard thumbnails (about 1 frame per second) or, for one video, frames from the downloaded file.
- **[M]** Metadata only: title, description, date, views. Not watched.
Dates, durations and view counts come from yt-dlp metadata pulled on 2026-10-23 for the channel https://www.youtube.com/@iugu. View counts are a snapshot from that day.

## 1. What advertising or brand campaigns has iugu run?

### Takeaway
I found no iugu ad campaign credited to an agency, no TV or out-of-home media buy, and no award for creative work in the advertising press (Meio & Mensagem, Propmark, Adnews, B9, Behance searches returned nothing on iugu). iugu's "campaigns" are in-house brand moments released on YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn: the **10-year rebrand "tecnologia que vira potência"** (April 2022), the **"Chegou a nova iugu!" / "infraestrutura sem limites" repositioning** (April–May 2025), the **employer-brand "#JornadaPotente" manifesto** (June 2026), and the **current ecosystem spots** (Feb and Sept 2026). The most recent creative piece is the 2:44 "iugu, o ecossistema mais completo e flexível de tecnologia financeira" video of 16 Sept 2026.

### Inferences
- iugu has three campaign "eras", each with its own look:
  - 2018–2021: teal identity, explainer voice-overs and the promise of "automação financeira".
  - April 2022 rebrand: periwinkle blue, "potência" concept, cinematic manifesto.
  - April 2025 to now: navy/blue/black, pill-label kinetic type with stair-step connectors, "ecossistema sem limites", hard numbers like R$81 bi and Top 10 Pix.
- A Reels script that fits the brand today should use the 2025–2026 visual grammar and keep "tecnologia que vira potência" as the sign-off.
- The 2022 manifesto's physics idea (potência = energia ÷ tempo; repetitive tasks drain energy) is the only true creative "big idea" in iugu's history. It compresses well into 30 seconds and could be retold as is.
- The Sept 2026 spot's structure ("O problema nem sempre está nas vendas, mas no que acontece depois delas" → four pain points → "É por isso que a iugu existe") is already a Reels script skeleton.
- The anonymous client in "Novo capítulo" who went from 40% to almost 90% card conversion matches the published Ballroom case (40% to 93.5%; see section 3). That is probably the same story, but it is not confirmed.
- The "R$81 bi" figure is worded two ways: "transacionados em 2024" (Feb 2026 spot) and "processados por ano" (Sept 2026 spot, LinkedIn). A script should use the wording of the latest source and avoid adding a year.

### Gaps
- I found no agency, production company or director credit for any iugu film. They appear to be in-house or uncredited. Not confirmed.
- I found no paid-media data (TV, OOH, Meta/Google ad spend) and no creative awards (Cannes, CCSP, El Ojo, etc.). The Meta Ad Library was not checked.
- The voice-over of "A iugu mudou para melhor!", "Chegou a nova iugu!" and the 2026 spots is unknown. They have no captions and seem to be music plus on-screen text, but I did not hear the audio.

**Ballroom (high-end party venues, 15+ venues across Brazil; client since 2018)**

## 5. Competitor creative for contrast (Asaas, Pagar.me/Stone, Vindi, Stripe Brasil)

### Takeaway
The category's big-budget ads use **TV celebrities plus an "elevation/rise" metaphor** (Asaas) or **real entrepreneurs plus actors and courage narratives** (Stone, parent of Pagar.me), with national TV, airport and sports media. ### Inferences
- Visual and tonal white space for iugu:
  - Competitors with budget lean on famous faces and "subir/voar" metaphors aimed at small business owners.
  - iugu's own assets point to a different lane: kinetic type with hard numbers, a CFO/back-office protagonist, "o problema está depois da venda", and "potência" as energy over time.
- A 30-second iugu Reel that copies the celebrity format would break with its history. A sharp problem-first, typography-led or real-client piece would fit it.
